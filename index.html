Tap Study in the bottom navigation bar → Find Tutors. Use the subject filter chips to narrow results. Tap a tutor card to view their full profile, rating, hourly rate, and weekly availability. Tap Book Session to request a tutoring session.

2Creating or joining study groups

From the StudyBuddy home screen, tap Study Groups. Tap + to create a new group — give it a name, subject, description, and a maximum member cap. To join an existing group, browse the list and tap Join. Inside a group you'll find tabs for Members, Sessions, and a Discussion chat.

✓After a tutoring session is marked complete, you'll be prompted to leave a star rating and review for your tutor. This helps other students find great tutors.
3Using Buddy AI Help

From the StudyBuddy home, tap Buddy AI Help. Type any academic question — for example, "Explain the chain rule" or "How does osmosis work?" — and buddy-bot will respond with clear, step-by-step explanations. See the buddy-bot section for more.

CampusMarket — Buy, Sell & Donate
A verified peer-to-peer marketplace for buying, selling, or donating goods within your university community. All users are verified students.

Tap Market in the bottom bar → Browse Listings. Use the category chips to filter by Electronics, Books, Furniture, Clothing, Sports, or Other. Tap any listing to view full details and contact the seller.

2Posting an item for sale or donation

Tap Post an Item from the CampusMarket home. Choose Sell an Item or Donate for Free. Upload up to 5 photos, enter a title, select a category and condition, set a price (sale only), and add a description. Tap Preview Listing, then Publish Listing.

!All commerce on CampusMarket is listing + negotiation only. Payments are handled offline between parties. Never share financial details in the in-app chat.
3Messaging a seller & leaving a review

On a listing page, tap the message icon to open an in-app conversation with the seller. Once a transaction is complete, both parties are prompted to leave a star rating (1–5) and written review. Ratings build the seller's Trust Engine score visible to the whole community.

Tap Housing in the bottom bar → Browse Listings. Filter by property type, location, price range, and bedroom count. Tap a listing to see full details including map location, amenities, and available-from date. Tap the heart icon to save a listing.

2Finding a compatible roommate

From the Housing Hub home, tap Roommate Match. Tap My Prefs to set your roommate preferences — budget range, preferred location, sleep schedule, cleanliness standard, noise level, and whether you're okay with pets or smoking. Tap Save & Find Matches to see compatible verified student profiles.

3Setting up housing alerts

Tap My Alerts from the Housing Hub home and toggle the Housing Module on. You'll receive automatic notifications whenever a new listing is posted that matches your saved preferences — area, maximum price, or property type. No need to keep checking manually.

✓All accommodation listings on Housing Hub are verified and added by the CampusBuddy team, not by anonymous third parties.

EventBoard — Campus Events
Discover upcoming campus events, register your attendance, create your own events, and never miss an event with automatic reminders.
1Discovering events & RSVP

Tap Events in the bottom bar. Browse by category (Academic, Social, Sports, Career) or view the Event Calendar. Tap an event card to see full details, then tap RSVP to register. If an event is full, you'll join the waitlist automatically.

2Saving events & setting reminders

On any event detail page, tap the bookmark icon to save the event to your personal list. Tap the bell icon to set a reminder — you'll receive an automatic notification 24 hours and 1 hour before the event starts.

3Creating your own event

From the EventBoard home, tap Create Event. Upload a banner image, enter the event title and description, select a category, set start and end date/time, enter the venue, choose the mode (In-Person, Online, or Hybrid), and optionally set a maximum capacity. Tap Publish Event to make it live for all students to discover.

buddy-bot — Your AI Study Assistant
buddy-bot is an always-available AI assistant powered by OpenAI. Ask it academic questions, request explanations, or get campus platform guidance.
1How to access buddy-bot

You can reach buddy-bot in two ways:

🏠Home screen— tap the floating robot button
📚StudyBuddy— tap "Buddy AI Help"
2What buddy-bot can help with
buddy-bot — Chat

buddy-bot is designed for academic questions, concept explanations, worked examples, code help, essay guidance, and general campus platform navigation. It is not connected to real-time internet data.

3Tips for better results
ℹBe specific — instead of "explain calculus", try "Explain the chain rule with a worked example using f(x) = (3x+1)²". Specific questions get more useful answers.

Each conversation with buddy-bot is independent — it doesn't remember previous chats. If you need context from an earlier session, include it in your new message.

🛡️  Trust Engine
Trust Engine — Verified & Accountable
The Trust Engine is CampusBuddy's built-in accountability system. It ensures every person you interact with is a verified student and has a visible reputation.
1University email verification

When you register, CampusBuddy checks that your email domain matches an approved university. A one-time OTP code is sent — only students who verify this code can post listings, message peers, or access full platform features. Unverified accounts have restricted access.

ℹLook for the blue verified badge on user profiles. This means the student has confirmed their university email.
2Peer ratings & reviews
VN
Victoria Ndicu ✓ Verified
Software Engineering · Year 4

After each qualifying interaction — a completed tutoring session, marketplace transaction, or housing arrangement — you'll be prompted to rate the other person from 1–5 stars. These ratings aggregate into a public profile score that future peers can see before deciding to engage.

3Reporting a concern

If you encounter a listing or interaction that seems fraudulent or inappropriate, tap the Report option on any listing or profile page. The platform team reviews all reports. For urgent concerns, use the Feedback page — select Report an Issue as the feedback type.

!Never share personal financial information (bank account details, M-Pesa PINs) in CampusBuddy chats. The platform does not process payments.
